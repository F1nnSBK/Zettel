## Academic Verification & Key Strengths for Graduate Admissions

1. **Full 210 ECTS Credits:** Meets and exceeds standard European (180 ECTS) and North American 4-year B.Sc. credit requirements without pre-requisite deficits.
2. **Heavy Mathematical & Theoretical Rigor:** Complete coursework in Linear Algebra, Numerical Optimization, Stochastics, Theoretical CS (P vs NP, Automata), Cryptography, and Deep Learning Theory.
3. **Systems & Data Infrastructure Focus:** Extensively covers High-Performance Data Processing, CUDA, Vector Engines, Cloud Architectures (GCP, BigQuery, GraalVM), and MLOps.
4. **Cooperative Industry Experience:** 48 ECTS of direct applied industry experience at dual study partner enterprises (Schwäbischer Verlag, Waldner Holding, Hertsch Technologies UG).
